## Head Stereo Camera

The head stereo camera (ZED mini) is optional.
You need it for `ROBOT=cell` in data collection.
See [Setup](../../tutorial/setup.mdx#3-check-the-vendor-and-product-id-for-the-head-stereo-camera) to use it.

- The parts to buy are listed in the "Head Camera" section of the BOM.
- The 3D CAD data is in the [Options](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ZJhmpndp_9CWCtUnLToTrYCXSu-fMuxj) folder:
  - `OpenArm_2.0_w_Head_Camera.STEP`: The assembly with the head stereo camera
  - `OpenArm_2.0_Head_Cover.STEP` and `OpenArm_2.0_Chest_Cover.STEP`: The covers to 3D print.
    The head cover has two bodies, and you need both.
    The chest cover has two bodies, too.
    Body 1 (back) is the same as the one in `OpenArm_2.0.STEP`, and body 2 (front) is for the head stereo camera.
    If you already printed the chest cover without the head stereo camera, print only body 2.

Attach the head stereo camera before the front chest cover.
The front chest cover has a large slit on the top so that it can be attached after the camera.
The slit is intentional.
There is no lid to cover it yet.
If you have an idea to improve it, share it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/FsZaZ4z3We) or [GitHub](https://github.com/enactic/openarm/issues).

:::warning

The head cover and the chest cover released before 2026-11-09 interfere with the camera mount.
If you have trouble attaching them, print them again with the current files.
If you could assemble the old ones, you can keep using them.

The covers in `OpenArm_2.0_w_Head_Camera.STEP` are still the old ones.
Use `OpenArm_2.0_Head_Cover.STEP` and `OpenArm_2.0_Chest_Cover.STEP` for printing.

:::

## General Dimensions

OpenArm 2.0 has a small human-like physique.
The support pillars are made of MiSUMi aluminum frames, making it easy to adjust the dimensions and attach cameras, sensors, and other attachments.
The base plate has evenly spaced M6 taps, allowing it to be fixed directly to a table or other surface.
Each joint has a mechanical limit that restricts the range of motion for safety.

<BlockImage src="hardware/openarm-2.0/general/v2_mech_stop_conf.png" alt="OpenArm 2.0 Dimensions" />

## Payload definition

### Nominal payload : 4.1kg

Nominal payload means the weight that could be held for one minute in the worst posture (arms extended to maximum).

<BlockImage src="hardware/openarm-2.0/general/nominal-payload.png" alt="OpenArm 2.0 Nominal Payload" />

### Peak payload : 6.0kg

Peak payload means the weight that could be moved from the arms down to the worst posture over a period of 3 seconds and held for 1 second before returning.

<BlockImage src="hardware/openarm-2.0/general/peak-payload.png" alt="OpenArm 2.0 Peak Payload" />

:::warning

Note that the payload contains the end effector. For example, if a 1.5 kg end-effector is attached to the OpenArm 2.0,
the nominal payload is 2.6 kg and the peak payload is 4.5 kg.

:::
